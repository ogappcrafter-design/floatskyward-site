Who She Was to Us

In memory of Jackie Carol Jones

My grandmother was a woman of deep faith, quiet strength, and a kindness that noticed people's needs before they said a word. She was steady when things got hard, she made everyone around her feel seen and valued, and she never lost her sense of humor. Her love was the kind that made a child feel safe, and it still shapes the way we treat people.

She taught me that you can be gentle and still be strong, and that joy is worth protecting even when life is heavy. Every time I build something, I try to bring that same care to it.

My grandmother lived with hope and never let the weight of hard days keep her from looking up. "Floating skyward" is what that looks like: rising above the heaviness, carrying others with you, and keeping the fun airborne. That's the spirit she gave us, and it's the spirit this studio is built on.
